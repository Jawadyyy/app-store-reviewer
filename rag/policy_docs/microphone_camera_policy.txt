Google Play Microphone and Camera Policy
==========================================

1. USER INITIATED: Apps must only access the microphone
   or camera when the user has explicitly triggered the
   feature. No background recording or capturing is allowed.

2. VISIBLE INDICATOR: Whenever the microphone or camera
   is active, a clear visual indicator must be shown to
   the user. This can be a recording icon or status bar
   notification.

3. NO BACKGROUND ACCESS: Apps must not record audio or
   capture camera footage when running in the background.
   Any attempt to do so will result in immediate removal
   from the Play Store.

4. DISCLOSURE: Microphone and camera usage must be
   clearly disclosed in the app's privacy policy and
   in the Play Store listing.

5. MINIMAL ACCESS: Apps should only request microphone
   or camera permissions if the feature is essential to
   the app's core functionality. Permission requests
   must be contextual and timed to when the feature is
   actually needed.

6. DATA HANDLING: Any audio or video captured must be
   handled securely. Developers must disclose how this
   data is stored, transmitted, and whether it is shared
   with third parties.
